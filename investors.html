Investor modules

Your workspace

Navigate the portal with six modules.

📊

Dashboard

Total invested, companies, applications submitted, founders in portfolio, portal users, total deployed, average ownership and average investment — with Portfolio Value Over Time and MOIC charts.

📰

My Activities

A feed of company updates — funding milestones, quarterly updates and requests — with a recent-activity panel and a place to share your own activity.

🏢

Companies

Your portfolio in grid or list view: industry, stage, status, amount invested, ownership % and location. Search, refresh, add, edit or remove companies.

📥

Applications

The Investment Decision queue. Filter by All, Ready for Decision, Approved or Rejected and open any application for the full review.

👥

Portal Users

Manage the founders and users invited to the Zoho VC CRM portal.

📁

Documents

Keep company documents in one place and request additional documents from founders during review.

FAQ

Common questions

Questions about Zoho VC CRM access or workflows? Reach out to your Zoho VC contact.

What do I see after signing in?+

The Dashboard: portfolio KPIs, performance analytics, recent portfolio companies, the pipeline funnel and funding ask by industry.

What does Ready for Decision mean?+

The application has cleared all three review levels and is waiting for a final approve or reject decision.

What is AI Scoring?+

An AI Assessment that scores an application against a fixed rubric, available on each application.

Can I add portfolio companies myself?+

Yes. The Companies module lets you add, edit and remove companies.
