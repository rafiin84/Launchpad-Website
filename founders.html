Our Ethos
Founders-first capital: more than the first check. The first faith.

Risk capital shared is strength, not burden. We back founders who build for the long term, investing in honest failures that lead to proud successes. Founders keep decision-making authority while we provide supportive guidance that encourages healthy risk-taking. We are the invisible guardrail.

The 3 G's

Governance. Growth. Grit.

Three guiding principles woven into every portfolio company.

G

Governance

Quiet discipline that keeps a company honest: clean books, clear boards and durable decision-making.

G

Growth

Healthy, not hasty. We back durable compounding over vanity spikes and inflated valuations.

G

Grit

Staying through the difficult quarters. Honest failures that lead to proud successes.

FAQ

Common questions

Questions about Zoho VC CRM access or workflows? Reach out to your Zoho VC contact.

How do I get access?+

Founder access is by invitation. Sign in with the account you were invited with.

Who sees my documents?+

Documents you upload are shared with your investor. Your investor may also upload documents for you to review.

How will I know the status of my application?+

My Application shows your progress through Level 1, Level 2, Level 3 and the final decision, plus any messages and scheduled meetings from your investor.

What does Update KPIs do?+

It lets you keep MRR, runway, total raised, burn rate, customers, growth, churn and team size current on your dashboard.
