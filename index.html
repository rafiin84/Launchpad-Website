Shortlisting Pipeline

Three review levels, one clear decision

Every application clears three levels before a final decision. Each level records a reviewer, a timestamp, a rating out of 5 and a comment, so the full trail is visible on the application.

  • Level 1 — Initial Screening: first-pass eligibility and fit check
  • Level 2 — Detailed Review: business model, traction and financial deep-dive
  • Level 3 — Final Shortlist: investment committee recommendation
  • Final Decision: approved or rejected, with progress shown as Step 1–4 of 4

FAQ

Common questions

Questions about Zoho VC CRM access or workflows? Reach out to your Zoho VC contact.

Who is Zoho VC CRM for?+

Investors use it to review applications, manage portfolio companies and follow activity. Founders use the portal to submit applications and keep investors informed.

How do applications get decided?+

Each application moves through Level 1 Initial Screening, Level 2 Detailed Review and Level 3 Final Shortlist. Once all three levels are cleared it is ready for a final decision — approved or rejected.

What can I see in the portfolio?+

For each company: industry, stage, status, amount invested, ownership % and location, in list or grid view.

How do I get access?+

Zoho VC CRM is invite-only. Founders and users are invited to the portal and sign in with their invited account.
